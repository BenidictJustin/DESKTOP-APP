# DommUnity: Definition of Terms

---

## Overview

For a clearer understanding of this study, the following terms are defined operationally and conceptually based on their specific functions within the **DommUnity** management system and the administrative procedures of the **Community Extension & Services (CES) Office** of Dominican College of Tarlac, Inc.

---

## Alphabetical List of Terms

### Catholic Education Association of the Philippines (CEAP) Advocacy Areas
The standardized framework of Catholic social teachings (e.g., Justice and Peace, Ecological Integrity, Poverty Alleviation, Gender Equality, and Youth Empowerment) adopted by the CES Office to classify and evaluate institutional outreach programs.

### Community Extension & Services (CES) Office
The administrative department of Dominican College of Tarlac, Inc. responsible for planning, executing, managing donations for, and documenting community outreach, volunteerism, and social responsibility initiatives across all academic departments.

### Desktop-Based Management System
A standalone computer application installed and operated directly on a local machine to handle record-keeping, inventory monitoring, and automated document compilation without relying on an external web browser interface.

### Donation Batch
A transaction record within the Donor Management Module that groups multiple donated items (e.g., food packs, hygiene kits, school supplies) under a single donor profile, recording contribution dates, intended beneficiaries, and item expiration dates.

### DommUnity
The customized desktop-based management system developed specifically for the CES Office of Dominican College of Tarlac, Inc. to streamline activity scheduling, donation inventory tracking, user access management, and automated narrative report generation.

### Expiration Tracking
An automated inventory feature that monitors the shelf life of consumable goods, prioritizing items nearing expiration for immediate distribution to avoid spoilage and waste.

### First In, First Out (FIFO)
An inventory management principle implemented in DommUnity ensuring that older batches of donated goods and relief supplies are staged and released first before newer inventory.

### Low Stock Threshold
A predetermined inventory level in DommUnity (set at ten units) that automatically triggers a status alert to notify administrators of dwindling supplies before an item reaches zero ("Out of Stock").

### Narrative Report
A standardized post-activity institutional document compiled by DommUnity that consolidates event objectives, beneficiary metrics, qualitative descriptions, and a maximum of ten activity photos into an official pre-formatted template.

### Peopleware
The human component involved in the operation and maintenance of the system, comprising the student researchers/developers and authorized institutional stakeholders (System Administrator, CES Head, and Department Coordinators).

### Report Review Workflow
The multi-tiered administrative lifecycle in DommUnity through which a narrative report progresses: drafted and submitted by a Coordinator, evaluated by the CES Admin (with options to approve or return with revision feedback), and finalized for archival.

### Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
A security feature within User Account Management that restricts system privileges and navigation according to assigned user roles (System Administrator, CES Head/Admin, and Department Coordinators).
